# Pet Connect — App Features Guide

## Nearby tab
Shows pets around you sorted by compatibility. Includes top match, available-now rail, category filters, and distance-aware cards.

## Discover tab
Swipe deck for expressing interest or skipping. Mutual interest creates a match.

## Listings tab
Marketplace for adopt and sell posts with photos, breed, age, price (sales), and location.

## Chats tab
List of matched conversations with unread counts and last message previews.

## Profile tab
Your owner profile, pets, location refresh, and breeding availability controls.

## Safety features
Report and block from chat. Approximate location sharing. Availability visibility gates. No exact GPS reveal in messages.
