# Dog Breeds Knowledge Base

## Labrador Retriever
Q: labrador | labrador retriever | lab dog | yellow lab | black lab
A: Labrador Retrievers are friendly, energetic, and great with families. Medium-large size (25–36 kg), high exercise needs, typically good with other dogs. Coat: short and dense. Common colors: yellow, black, chocolate. Lifespan ~10–12 years. On Pet Connect, Labs often score well with other sporting breeds nearby.

## Golden Retriever
Q: golden retriever | golden | goldie
A: Golden Retrievers are gentle, trainable, and affectionate. Similar size to Labs, love swimming and fetch, need daily exercise and grooming for their longer coat. Excellent companion and family dogs. Watch for hip and ear issues as they age.

## German Shepherd
Q: german shepherd | gsd | alsatian
A: German Shepherds are loyal, intelligent, and protective. Large (30–40 kg), high training and exercise needs. Best with experienced owners. Double coat sheds heavily. Strong territorial instincts — introduce carefully to other dogs on Pet Connect.

## Beagle
Q: beagle | beagle dog
A: Beagles are curious scent hounds, medium-small (9–11 kg), pack-oriented and vocal. Need secure fencing and mental stimulation. Generally good with other dogs. Short coat, easy grooming. Average lifespan 12–15 years.

## Pug
Q: pug | pug dog
A: Pugs are compact companion dogs with flat faces. Low exercise but care with heat and breathing. Friendly and people-oriented. Brachycephalic — avoid overheating. Great apartment pets; check vaccination and health notes carefully when matching.

## Indie / Indian Pariah Dog
Q: indie dog | indian street dog | pariah | desi dog | indian mongrel
A: Indie (Indian Pariah) dogs are hardy, intelligent, and adapted to local climates. Medium size, low grooming, strong immunity when well cared for. Very common on Pet Connect in Indian cities. Temperament varies — meet and assess compatibility in person after matching.

## Pomeranian
Q: pomeranian | pom | fluffy small dog
A: Pomeranians are tiny (1.5–3 kg), alert, and fluffy. High grooming needs, can be barky. Best with careful handling around larger dogs. Great for apartment living with daily walks.

## Siberian Husky
Q: husky | siberian husky
A: Huskies are energetic working dogs needing high exercise and cool climates. Thick double coat, pack-oriented, known escape artists. Not ideal for sedentary homes. Introduce carefully; they may have strong prey drive.

## Bulldog (English)
Q: bulldog | english bulldog
A: English Bulldogs are calm companions with short muzzles. Low stamina, heat-sensitive, need specialised care. Short coat. Breeding should only involve responsible, health-screened owners — Pet Connect is for connections, not veterinary advice.

## Rottweiler
Q: rottweiler | rottie
A: Rottweilers are powerful, loyal guardian dogs. Need firm training and socialisation. Large and strong — not for first-time owners. Match with similar-size, well-socialised dogs when using Pet Connect.

## Shih Tzu
Q: shih tzu | shihtzu
A: Shih Tzus are small companion dogs with long coats needing regular grooming. Affectionate and adaptable to apartments. Moderate exercise. Friendly but can be stubborn in training.

## Dachshund
Q: dachshund | sausage dog | wiener dog
A: Dachshunds are long-bodied scent hounds. Watch their backs — avoid jumping from heights. Bold personality in a small body. Short or long coat varieties. Good with careful handling around larger breeds.

## Boxer
Q: boxer dog | boxer
A: Boxers are playful, energetic, and loyal. Medium-large, short coat, need firm training. Generally good with families. Can be boisterous with smaller dogs — supervise early meetups after matching.

## Cocker Spaniel
Q: cocker spaniel | spaniel
A: Cocker Spaniels are affectionate sporting dogs with silky coats needing grooming. Medium size, love activity and companionship. Usually friendly with other dogs when socialised.

## Doberman
Q: doberman | dobermann
A: Dobermans are sleek, athletic, and highly trainable. Need experienced handlers and lots of exercise. Short coat. Protective instincts — structured introductions on Pet Connect are important.

## Dog size categories
Q: small dog breeds | medium dog breeds | large dog breeds | dog sizes
A: Rough guide — Small: Pug, Pomeranian, Shih Tzu, Dachshund. Medium: Beagle, Cocker Spaniel, Indie. Large: Labrador, Golden Retriever, German Shepherd, Husky, Rottweiler, Boxer, Doberman. Size affects safe play and housing when matching companions.

## Dog exercise needs
Q: which dogs need lots of exercise | high energy dogs | lazy dogs
A: High energy: Husky, German Shepherd, Labrador, Boxer, Doberman. Moderate: Beagle, Indie, Golden, Cocker. Lower: Pug, Bulldog, Shih Tzu, Pomeranian (still need daily walks). Match energy levels for happier pairings.

## Dog grooming
Q: dog grooming | shedding dogs | hypoallergenic dogs
A: Heavy shedders: Husky, German Shepherd, Labs, Goldens. High grooming: Pomeranian, Shih Tzu, Cocker. Low grooming: Pug, Beagle, short-coated Indie. True hypoallergenic dogs are rare — discuss allergies before meetings.

## Puppy vs adult
Q: puppy vs adult dog | young dog breeding
A: Pets on Pet Connect should use real date of birth. Breeding availability should only apply to healthy, mature animals — never puppies. Compatibility scoring uses age closeness between pets.

## Dog vaccination basics
Q: dog vaccines | rabies dog | puppy shots
A: Core vaccines typically include rabies and distemper/parvo combinations — follow your vet. Pet Connect shows vaccinated / partial / not vaccinated so owners can decide. This is not veterinary advice.
