# Pet Connect — General FAQ

## What is Pet Connect?
Q: what is pet connect | what does this app do | tell me about the app
A: Pet Connect helps pet owners find compatible companions nearby. Create a profile for your pet, browse pets around you, match on mutual interest, chat once matched, and post pets for adoption or sale.

## How does matching work?
Q: how does matching work | how do matches work | mutual interest
A: On Discover, swipe on pets you like. When both owners show interest in each other's pets, you get a match and can chat. Compatibility scores consider breed, age, gender, distance, and health signals.

## What is the compatibility score?
Q: compatibility score | how is score calculated | what does % mean
A: The compatibility score (0–100%) weighs breed similarity, age closeness, complementary gender for breeding interest, distance, and vaccination/health factors. Higher scores mean a stronger nearby match for your pet.

## How do I add my pet?
Q: how do i add a pet | create pet profile | register my dog | register my cat
A: Go to Profile → add a pet. You'll set species, breed, gender, date of birth, photos, bio, vaccination status, and whether they're open to breeding. Location is taken from your device (or a city fallback).

## Breeding availability
Q: breeding availability | open to breeding | availability window | heat cycle
A: You can mark a pet as available for breeding and set a date window. Visibility can be private, matches only, or nearby owners — so reproductive details stay under your control.

## Privacy and location
Q: is my location private | do you share my address | approximate location
A: Exact addresses are never shown. Distances are calculated from your location, and when you share location in chat it's blurred to about a 1 km radius.

## Listings — adopt and sell
Q: how do listings work | adopt a pet | sell a pet | marketplace
A: Listings let owners post pets for adoption (no price) or sale (price in rupees). Browse on the Listings tab, open a listing for details, and contact the owner through the app flow.

## Chat
Q: how does chat work | when can i message | send photos in chat
A: Chat unlocks after a mutual match. You can send text, images, and an approximate location. You can also report or block an owner from the chat menu.

## Notifications
Q: what notifications do i get | alerts | nearby available
A: You'll get notified about new matches, messages, nearby pets that become available, availability reminders, and listing responses.

## Filters
Q: how do filters work | filter by breed | distance filter
A: On Nearby, open Filters to limit by distance, species, breed, gender, age range, and "available for breeding only."

## Spayed or neutered
Q: can spayed pets breed | neutered breeding
A: Pets marked as spayed or neutered are not suitable for breeding matches. The app still lets you browse and list them for companionship context, but breeding availability should stay off.

## Vaccination
Q: why vaccination status | vaccinated partial
A: Vaccination status (vaccinated, partial, or not vaccinated) feeds into the health part of compatibility and helps other owners make informed decisions.

## Who can see my pet?
Q: who can see my pet | is my profile public
A: Nearby owners can see pets in their radius (subject to filters). Breeding windows respect the visibility you set. Blocked owners cannot reach you in chat.

## Support
Q: contact support | report a problem | help desk
A: Use the menu → Support for help with account issues, reporting misuse, or app questions. For safety concerns, report and block from chat.

## Account
Q: how do i sign up | login | create account
A: Use the login/signup screens to create an owner account, then add your pets from Profile.
